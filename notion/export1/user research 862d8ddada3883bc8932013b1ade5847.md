# UX Research Methodology

## Foundational Research

### Why Qualitative Interviews?

For this project, I prioritized qualitative data to guide the MVP scope. Leveraging my role as Administrator of Neo's Discord server, I have direct access to an active, high-intent community. This "human capital" allows me to conduct in-depth semi-structured interviews. The goal is to go beyond surface-level data (the *what*) and uncover deep motivations, emotional drivers, and friction points (the *why*) behind the fan experience.

### Secondary Research (Competitive Analysis)

To complement primary research, I'm conducting a competitive audit analyzing the digital ecosystems of prominent artists and YouTubers. This benchmark helps identify:

- Industry standards for art navigation
- Common UI patterns in creator marketplaces
- Unexplored opportunities to differentiate Neo's brand

# 🔬 UX Research Plan

## 1. Research Goals

| **Goal** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Map the Journey** | Understand the emotional and practical process fans go through when seeking Neo's content |
| **Identify Friction** | Pinpoint usability issues and fragmentation across current platforms |
| **Define MVP Requirements** | Validate user expectations for a centralized hub, ensuring the "Happy Path" aligns with business goals |

## 2. Target Participants

| **Criteria** | **Details** |
| --- | --- |
| **Demographics** | 18–30 years old (Discord community skews 19-24; this range represents Neo's active fanbase) |
| **Behavior** | Active Discord members (Power Users) and long-time YouTube subscribers |
| **Inclusion Strategy** | While recruiting from a specific community (potential Affinity Bias), I'm selecting participants with diverse technical abilities and backgrounds to ensure broader accessibility perspectives |

## 3. Method: Semi-Structured Interviews

To truly empathize with the audience, I chose semi-structured interviews. This choice transforms the Discord server into a high-density "human lab." Following the Google UX Design framework, the script is designed to be:

| **Principle** | **Implementation** |
| --- | --- |
| **Open-ended** | Encourages storytelling rather than binary responses |
| **Neutral** | Eliminates interviewer bias to obtain authentic data |
| **Iterative** | Uses probing follow-up questions to dig into pain points |

**Structure:** Icebreaker (Rapport) → Behavioral Mapping (Habits) → Value Discovery (Needs)

# 🗣️ Interview Structure (30-35 minutes)

---

## Phase 1: Building Rapport

**Goal:** Make the participant comfortable and establish context

### Opening Script

"Thank you for your time! Today I want to better understand your experience as a Neo fan. There are no right or wrong answers—I'm only interested in your authentic perspective."

### Questions

| **Question** | **Follow-up / Probe For** |
| --- | --- |
| "How did you first discover Neo? Tell me about that moment." | • What caught your attention enough to keep following? |
| "Are you part of Neo's active community in comments or on Discord?" | **If YES:** "What's your favorite memory related to Neo's community?"                                                                           **If NO:** "What stops you from participating?" |
| "What makes this community special compared to other artists you follow?" |  |

---

## Phase 2: Current Behavior & Pain Points

**Goal:** Map the existing user journey and identify friction points

### A. Content Discovery & Consumption

| **Question** | • **Follow-up / (Probe For)** |
| --- | --- |
| "During the week, how do you typically interact with Neo's content?" | • Which platforms do you open first? Why that one and not others?                                                                                 • How often do you check for updates? Is it a habit or does it happen randomly? |
| "Tell me about the last time you searched for a specific Neo drawing you'd seen before." | • Where did you search? How long did it take?              • How did you feel during that search? (frustrated, neutral, excited?)                                                                    • **Apply 5 Whys:** "Why was it important to find that specific drawing?" |

### B. Art Exploration

| **Question** | **Follow-up / Probe For** |
| --- | --- |
| "What emotions do you feel when you look at Neo's art as a whole?" |  |
| "When you look at a piece of Neo's artwork, which details do you focus on most?" | • Do current platforms (Instagram, YouTube) let you appreciate these details as you'd like? |
| "If you had to describe Neo's style with three adjectives to someone who doesn't know him, which would you use?" | • What's the first thing you would share about Neo to introduce him to your friends?                                               •  What part of yourself do you think Neo's art represents the most? |

### C. Events & Updates - FOMO - Engagement

| **Question** | **Follow-up / Probe For** |
| --- | --- |
| "Tell me about a time when you found out too late about a Neo live stream, event, or video." | • How did you react? What would you have wanted to do differently?                                                                        • How do you currently manage the fear of missing his updates? |
| "If you could know in advance Neo's commitments and projects, what information would make you feel more involved as a fan?" | **Probe for:** Exact dates vs. "coming soon," behind-the-scenes teasers, notification preferences |
| "And if you could peek into any aspect of Neo's life?" | **Note:** This tests boundaries between professional/personal content expectations |

---

## Phase 3: Commerce & Trust

**Goal:** Understand purchasing motivations and barriers

| **Question** | **Follow-up / Probe For** |
| --- | --- |
| "Have you ever bought art or merchandise from independent artists online? Tell me how it went." | • What made you feel confident (or uncertain) about the purchase?                                                                           • What made that transaction feel 'special' instead of just any purchase? |
| "Imagine you want to buy a print of a Neo drawing. Before clicking 'buy', what information do you look for on the page?" | **Probe for:** Price, dimensions, materials, story behind the artwork, limited edition info, shipping times |
| "When you think about owning a physical piece of Neo's art, what would make it an authentic 'piece of Neo' and not just an object?" | **Listen for:** Signature, certificate, packaging, personal touch, story |

---

## Phase 4: Music & Multimedia

**Goal:** Validate music section priority *(Research outcome: Validated as Phase 2 feature — 4/7 participants rated music as secondary to visual art)*

| **Question** | **Follow-up / Probe For** |
| --- | --- |
| "How important is the music in Neo's videos to you?" | • Have you ever wanted to re-listen to a specific song separately from the video? What did you do? |
| "If Neo's site had a section for his original music, how often do you think you'd visit it compared to the gallery?" | **Note:** This helps prioritize MVP features |

---

## Phase 5: Ideal Solution & Magic Wand

**Goal:** Uncover unmet needs and prioritize features

| **Question** | **Follow-up / Probe For** |
| --- | --- |
| "Imagine being teleported into Neo's universe, how it is?" | • How did you get there?                                                           • What's the first thing you see?                                           • What do you hear?                                                                                • How do you feel there? |
| "If Neo were a physical object, how would you imagine it?" | • Where is the object placed?                                                       • What sensation should it give to the touch, etc.?                                           • And if Neo were an environment instead?                                                                                                  **Note:** This reveals brand perception and desired aesthetic |
| "If tomorrow morning Neo's official site existed, what would be the very first thing you'd expect to find when you open it?" | • Why that specific thing?                                                  • Would it let you do something you can't today?                                                                              • "Magic wand: you can add ONE feature to Neo's site that doesn't exist anywhere today. What would it be?" |
| "If Neo's entire concept were a color, what color would it be?" | •  How do you feel imagining that color?                             •  What would that color be in your daily life? What use would it have? |

---

## Phase 6: Contact & Future Interaction

| **Question** | **Follow-up / Probe For** |
| --- | --- |
| "If you needed to contact Neo for a commission or question, how would you like to do that?" | **Probe for:** Form vs. direct email, expected response times, level of formality |

---

## Closing (2 min)

| **Final Question** | **Thank You** |
| --- | --- |
| "We're done! Is there anything else we haven't covered that you'd like to share?" | "Thank you so much for your time—your answers will help me tremendously to create an experience that truly works for the community." |
