## **What This Means for Design:**

| **Vision Element** | **Design Translation** |
| --- | --- |
| **Raw Authenticity** | No corporate polish—preserve the artist's unfiltered voice |
| **Dark Aesthetic** | Lean into shadows, contrast, and bold visual statements |
| **Independence** | Platform that operates outside traditional gatekeepers |

---

### Target Users (MVP Validated)

| **User Type** | **Why They Matter** | **What They Need** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fans** (Sabrina persona) | Core community driving engagement and revenue | Easy access to art, events, and merch; sense of belonging |
| **Event Organizers** (Elena persona) | Partners for live experiences and collaborations | Professional contact system, calendar visibility, event updates |

### Future Target Users (Post-MVP)

| **User Type** | **Why They Matter** | **What They Need** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Art Students** (Marco persona - partial) | Study Neo's techniques and artistic evolution | Searchable archive, high-res zoom, technique filters, process documentation |
| **Publishing Houses** | Potential clients for commercial work | Portfolio showcase, professional credentials, inquiry system |
| **Peers (Other Artists)** | Network for cross-promotion and community building | Inspiration, collaboration opportunities, transparent process |

---

### Desired Outcomes

| **Outcome** | **Success Metrics** | **Design Strategy** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Independence in Sales** | Direct revenue without third-party platforms (Etsy dependency) | Contact-based purchase system: gallery browsing → cart → email inquiry to Neo (no automated checkout in MVP) |
| **Brand Centralization** | Single source of truth for all Neo content | Unified hub linking all platforms, consistent visual identity, comprehensive archive |
| **Professional Credibility** | Inquiries from event organizers and publishers | Portfolio presentation, professional contact system, case studies/past work |
| **Community Strengthening** | Increased fan engagement and loyalty | Event calendar, exclusive content, behind-the-scenes access |

---

While Neo has a consolidated artistic identity, there's openness to user-centered design decisions. This creates an opportunity to validate aesthetic choices through research, ensuring the final design resonates with the target audience while maintaining artistic integrity.

---

### Design Requirements Summary

| **Category** | **Requirement** |
| --- | --- |
| **Functional Priority** | Independent e-commerce capability (replacing Etsy dependency) |
| **Visual Identity** | Dark aesthetic with high contrast, bold statements, raw authenticity (aligned with Danzig "Mother" vision) |
| **User Experience** | Immersive brand world that feels like entering "Neo's universe" |
| **Professional Tools** | Contact system for commissions, event calendar, portfolio showcase, e-commerce |
| **Community Features** | Event visibility, update notifications, fan engagement touchpoints |

**Core Philosophy:**

The website isn't just a digital storefront—it's Neo's artistic statement of independence. Every design decision must balance raw creative expression with strategic business goals, all while keeping the fan experience at the center.
