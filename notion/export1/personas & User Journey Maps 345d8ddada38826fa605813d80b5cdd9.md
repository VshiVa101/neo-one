### (3-2)

PRIMARY PERSONAS

*Three representative users guiding design decisions for Neo's website*

---

## Persona Selection Strategy

From 7 interviews and 4 user groups, I created **3 primary personas** representing the dominant behavioral patterns and needs:

1. **Marco "Scazzo" Bianchi** — Represents The Apprentice + The Resister (technical mastery + ideological rebellion)
2. **Sabrina Moretti** — Represents The Seeker + The Collector (emotional depth + authentic ownership)
3. **Elena Rossi** — Represents cross-cutting FOMO concerns affecting all groups (event-focused engagement)

These personas cover **all critical pain points** identified in research while avoiding redundancy.

# Persona 1: Marco "Scazzo" Bianchi

## "The Rebellious Apprentice"

![Visual: Young artist studying artwork on laptop late at night, Discord open in background]

---

## At a Glance

| **Attribute** | **Details** |
| --- | --- |
| **Age** | 19 years old |
| **Location** | Brescia, Italy |
| **Occupation** | Art Student (Fine Arts Academy) |
| **Tech Proficiency** | High — Discord power user, comfortable with all platforms |
| **Primary Device** | Smartphone (70%), Laptop (30%) |
| **Income** | €50-100/month disposable income |
| **Archetype** | Apprentice + Resister |

---

## User Story

> As an aspiring artist who values technical mastery and creative independence, I want to study Neo's uncensored artwork in extreme detail and participate in underground community projects, so that I can develop authentic skills outside the mainstream art establishment and feel validated in my anti-commercial approach to art.
> 

---

## Background

Marco lives with his parents in Brescia, where his bedroom doubles as his studio. He's enrolled in Fine Arts Academy but is deeply skeptical of mainstream art education, which he sees as too focused on commercial success and "style branding." He discovered Neo through YouTube's algorithm and was immediately drawn to the raw, uncompromising nature of the work. Marco spends 6-8 hours daily online—drawing, watching tutorials, and moderating Neo's Discord server where he's an admin.

**Quote:**

> "Neo goes straight ahead and tells you: it's not like that, I do whatever the fuck I want. If tomorrow I want to sing, I sing. That's what I need."
> 

---

## Goals & Motivations

| **Primary Goals** | **Secondary Goals** |
| --- | --- |
| • Master traditional drawing techniques (pen, chiaroscuro, cross-hatching)<br>• Develop authentic personal style without copying<br>• Participate in collaborative art projects | • Build connections with like-minded artists<br>• Stay informed about underground art scene<br>• Support independent artists when financially possible |

**Success looks like:** Has developed a unique style, participates in underground community, makes art full-time independently

---

## Pain Points & Frustrations

| **Category** | **Specific Pain Points** |
| --- | --- |
| **Technical** | • Can't zoom enough on Instagram to study line details<br>• High-res images unavailable for reference<br>• Video quality doesn't show technical precision |
| **Ideological** | • Social media censorship ruins artist's vision<br>• Mainstream platforms favor commercial/AI art<br>• "Underground" losing meaning in algorithm age |
| **Financial** | • Limited budget prevents buying artwork<br>• Etsy prices feel out of reach |
| **Discovery** | • Misses collaboration opportunities if announced late<br>• Events discovered too late to participate |

**Emotional Impact:** *"I want to learn but the system won't let me see what I need"*

---

## Behaviors & Platform Usage

| **Activity** | **Pattern** |
| --- | --- |
| **Content Consumption** | 2-3x per week, watches new videos same day, rewatches technique showcases, takes screenshots for reference |
| **Community Engagement** | Daily Discord activity (several hours), admin/moderator role, voice chats with other artists |
| **Platform Checking** | YouTube (primary) + Discord + Instagram (2x per week, methodical channel visits) |
| **Shopping Behavior** | Weekly Etsy window shopping, checks prices but gets discouraged, builds wishlists |

**Key Quote:**

> "I've copied that alien gun drawing a thousand times—it's super detailed, very cyberpunk. I need to see every line to understand the technique."
> 

---

## Website Needs (Priority Ranked)

| **Priority** | **Need** | **Why It Matters** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔴 **Critical** | High-res images with pixel-level zoom | "I need to see every line, every cross-hatch, every decision" |
| 🔴 **Critical** | Completely uncensored gallery | Social media censorship blocks his learning and violates artistic integrity |
| 🟠 **High** | Filterable gallery (by technique/medium/year) | Wants to study evolution of specific skills over time |
| 🟠 **High** | Event calendar with collaboration announcements | Misses opportunities due to fragmented announcements across platforms |
| 🟡 **Medium** | Process/behind-the-scenes content | Wants to understand methodology, not just final results |

**Deal-breaker:** No zoom functionality = complete failure for this user

---

## User Journey: "Trying to Study a Drawing"

| **Stage** | **Action** | **Emotion** | **Pain Point** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Discovery** | Sees drawing in YouTube video | 😃 Excited | Video compression limits detail |
| **Attempt 1** | Pauses video, tries to screenshot | 😐 Neutral | Can't see line quality clearly |
| **Attempt 2** | Searches Instagram for high-res version | 😤 Frustrated | Instagram doesn't allow zoom, image is cropped |
| **Attempt 3** | Checks Etsy to see if available as print | 😟 Worried | Too expensive, unclear if it shows detail |
| **Outcome** | Gives up, uses low-quality screenshot | 😞 Defeated | **→ Website need: High-res gallery with zoom** |

---

## Success Metrics

| **Metric** | **Success Indicator** |
| --- | --- |
| **Immediate Win** | Can zoom to see individual pen strokes |
| **Content Win** | Gallery is completely uncensored |
| **Learning Win** | Learns new technique from process documentation |
| **Participation Win** | Discovers collaboration opportunity early enough to participate |
| **Overall Success** | **"I'm learning and I'm part of this movement"** |

# Persona 2: Sabrina Moretti

## "The Immersive Seeker"

![Visual: Woman in tattoo studio, Neo's art on phone screen while working]

---

## At a Glance

| **Attribute** | **Details** |
| --- | --- |
| **Age** | 24 years old |
| **Location** | Florence, Italy |
| **Occupation** | Tattoo Artist / Freelance Illustrator |
| **Tech Proficiency** | Medium-high — Social media savvy, basic web, creative apps |
| **Primary Device** | Smartphone (90%), Desktop for work (10%) |
| **Income** | €500-800/month disposable income |
| **Archetype** | Seeker + Collector |

---

## User Story

> As a creative professional who seeks emotional depth and personal meaning in art, I want to immerse myself in Neo's complete artistic universe with rich context and storytelling, so that I can understand myself better through his work and feel confident investing in authentic pieces that carry his energy.
> 

---

## Background

Sabrina is Neo's partner and has followed his artistic journey intimately. She works as a tattoo artist and freelance illustrator in Florence, where she shares a creative studio space with Neo. Art serves as meditation and therapy for her—she draws daily and curates her physical space carefully with meaningful pieces. She discovered Neo through her brother and was immediately moved by the emotional depth and attention to detail in his work.

**Quote:**

> "His art changed my existence. It's a world that has truly transformed me as a person. I hope more people can feel this depth, this sensitivity—it's rare."
> 

---

## Goals & Motivations

| **Primary Goals** | **Secondary Goals** |
| --- | --- |
| • Find emotional resonance and personal meaning in art<br>• Understand the "why" behind artistic choices<br>• Immerse in complete artistic universe | • Own physical pieces that anchor her space<br>• Support artists whose vision aligns with hers<br>• Track artistic evolution over time |

**Success looks like:** Lives surrounded by meaningful art, feels emotionally fulfilled, maintains creative practice that feeds her soul

---

## Pain Points & Frustrations

| **Category** | **Specific Pain Points** |
| --- | --- |
| **Technical** | • Can't zoom enough on phone to see emotionally important details<br>• Instagram doesn't show context or evolution<br>• Censorship hides complete works |
| **Emotional** | • Feels one-way (viewing without understanding)<br>• No space to see others' interpretations<br>• Artist's voice sometimes lost in algorithm |
| **Commercial** | • Etsy feels cold and transactional<br>• Can't see packaging/presentation<br>• No personal touch from artist |
| **Discovery** | • No descriptions to understand deeper meaning<br>• Can't organize favorites meaningfully |

**Emotional Impact:** *"I'm missing the deeper meaning, the story behind the work"*

---

## Behaviors & Platform Usage

| **Activity** | **Pattern** |
| --- | --- |
| **Content Consumption** | Daily, videos as background while working, rewatches favorites for comfort, pauses to analyze and reflect |
| **Community Engagement** | Observes more than participates, reads comments to feel connected, shares selectively with close friends |
| **Platform Checking** | Instagram (daily morning/evening), YouTube (while working), returns to favorites when stressed |
| **Shopping Behavior** | Monthly browsing and dreaming, researches carefully, imagines pieces in her space |

**Key Quote:**

> "When I see one of his works, I dive in headfirst. Everything turns off around me and I'm there, trying to understand, but also to understand myself."
> 

---

## Website Needs (Priority Ranked)

| **Priority** | **Need** | **Why It Matters** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔴 **Critical** | Detailed descriptions for each artwork | "I need the story, the why, the meaning" |
| 🔴 **Critical** | Immersive, atmospheric design (90s aesthetic) | "I want to feel like I'm entering his world" |
| 🔴 **Critical** | High-resolution zoom capability | Wants to see emotional details, immerse in complexity |
| 🟠 **High** | Evolution timeline showing artistic growth | Wants to understand the journey, not just endpoints |
| 🟡 **Medium** | Transparent product presentation with materials/packaging info | "It needs to feel like it carries his energy" |

**Deal-breaker:** No context or descriptions = shallow experience

---

## User Journey: "Wanting to Own a Piece"

| **Stage** | **Action** | **Emotion** | **Pain Point** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Discovery** | Sees artwork that deeply moves her | 🥺 Longing | Doesn't know the story behind it |

| **Shopping** | Checks Etsy to see if available | 😐 Neutral | Feels transactional, cold |
| **Hesitation** | Wants to buy but unsure about authenticity | 😟 Worried | Can't see materials, packaging, or artist's touch |
| **Outcome** | Doesn't purchase, feels disconnected | 😔 Disappointed | **→ Website need: Emotional presentation with context** |

---

## Success Metrics

| **Metric** | **Success Indicator** |

# Persona 3: Elena Rossi

## "The Event Seeker"

![Visual: Young person checking phone anxiously, Discord notifications piling up]

---

## At a Glance

| **Attribute** | **Details** |
| --- | --- |
| **Age** | 21 years old |
| **Location** | Milan, Italy |
| **Occupation** | University Student (Communication Design) |
| **Tech Proficiency** | High — Gen Z digital native |
| **Primary Device** | Smartphone (95%), Laptop (5%) |
| **Income** | €100-200/month disposable income |
| **Archetype** | Cross-cutting (affects all groups) |

---

## User Story

> As a dedicated community member who fears missing important events, I want a centralized calendar with reliable notifications for all of Neo's upcoming streams, exhibitions, and collaborations, so that I can plan ahead, participate fully, and feel like a valued part of the community.
> 

---

## Background

Elena is a university student studying Communication Design in Milan. She's an active member of Neo's Discord community and considers herself a dedicated fan, but she constantly struggles with FOMO (fear of missing out) on events, live streams, and collaboration opportunities. She checks her phone multiple times daily but still misses announcements because they're scattered across Discord, YouTube community posts, and Instagram stories. She values being "in the know" and feels anxious when she discovers events after they've happened.

**Quote:**

> "I always find out too late. I check Discord every day but somehow I still miss when he announces a stream. It makes me feel like I'm not a real fan."
> 

---

## Goals & Motivations

| **Primary Goals** | **Secondary Goals** |
| --- | --- |
| • Never miss live streams, events, or collaboration opportunities<br>• Feel connected to the community<br>• Be part of the "inner circle" | • Support Neo directly (beyond platform algorithms)<br>• Participate in interactive projects<br>• Plan ahead for events she wants to attend |

**Success looks like:** Always knows what's happening, can plan participation, feels included and valued

---

## Pain Points & Frustrations

| **Category** | **Specific Pain Points** |
| --- | --- |
| **Discovery** | • Misses Discord announcements in busy channels<br>• Inconsistent notification timing across platforms<br>• No centralized event calendar |
| **Emotional** | • Feels left out when discovers events after they happened<br>• Anxious about missing important updates<br>• Questions her dedication as a fan |
| **Technical** | • Multiple platforms to check (YouTube, Instagram, Discord)<br>• No single source of truth for schedule |

**Emotional Impact:** *"I feel like I'm failing as a fan even though I check daily"*

---

## Behaviors & Platform Usage

| **Activity** | **Pattern** |
| --- | --- |
| **Content Consumption** | Daily checks across multiple platforms, sets notifications but still misses things |
| **Community Engagement** | Moderately active in Discord, wants to participate more but often too late |
| **Platform Checking** | YouTube (daily), Discord (multiple times daily), Instagram (daily), compulsive checking |
| **Event Behavior** | Tries to attend everything but often finds out too late to plan |

**Key Quote:**

> "I check Discord every day, YouTube notifications are on, I follow on Instagram—but I still miss things. It's so frustrating."
> 

---

## Website Needs (Priority Ranked)

| **Priority** | **Need** | **Why It Matters** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔴 **Critical** | Centralized event calendar with all upcoming dates | Single source of truth, no more platform fragmentation |
| 🔴 **Critical** | Advance notice (not real-time alerts) | Wants to plan ahead, not react last-minute |
| 🟠 **High** | iCal/Google Calendar export | Wants events in her personal calendar app |
| 🟡 **Medium** | Archive of past events | FOMO mitigation—can catch up on what she missed |

**Deal-breaker:** No centralized calendar = continued anxiety and missed opportunities

---

## User Journey: "Missing a Live Stream"

| **Stage** | **Action** | **Emotion** | **Pain Point** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Morning** | Checks Discord, YouTube, Instagram | 😐 Neutral | No announcements visible |
| **Afternoon** | Goes to class, phone on silent | 😊 Content | Unaware stream was announced |
| **Evening** | Sees Discord messages: "Great stream today!" | 😰 Panic | Realizes she missed it completely |
| **Research** | Scrolls back to find announcement | 😤 Frustrated | Was buried in busy Discord channel |
| **Outcome** | Feels excluded, questions her fandom | 😞 Defeated | **→ Website need: Advance event calendar** |

---

## Success Metrics

| **Metric** | **Success Indicator** |
| --- | --- |
| **Discovery Win** | Finds next event within 10 seconds of visiting site |
| **Planning Win** | Can add events to personal calendar with one click |
| **Participation Win** | Attends 90%+ of events she wants to attend (up from 40%) |
| **Emotional Win** | Feels confident and included, no more FOMO anxiety |
| **Overall Success** | **"I never miss anything important anymore"** |
