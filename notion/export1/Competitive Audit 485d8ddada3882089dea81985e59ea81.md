# Competitive Audit

## Analyze direct & indirect competitors to identify patterns, pain points, and opportunities for Neo-one's artist portfolio/commerce site

Research Date: January 20-21-22, 2026

---

# Executive Summary

### Key Findings

- 🚨 70% of target artists lack personal websites → rely on fragmented third-party platforms
- 💰 Commerce is outsourced (BigCartel, Etsy, Threadless) → poor brand cohesion
- 📱 Mobile experience highly inconsistent across existing sites
- 🎭 Brand voice varies wildly → no industry standard for dark/alternative artists
- ⚠️ Major pain point: Fragmented presence reduces discoverability & sales

# Key Takeaways for Neo-one

## Patterns to ADOPT

1. Masonry grid gallery (Miles Johnston model) → dynamic, familiar, showcases variety
2. Integrated commerce (James Jean model) → seamless UX, brand cohesion, higher conversion
3. Thematic organization (Chet Zar model) → "Collections" or "Series" for complex portfolios
4. News/Blog section (Hollow Press/Fantagraphics) → own your announcements, don't rely on social
5. Mobile-first performance (James Jean/Miles Johnston) → fast load, responsive, optimized images
6. Comprehensive metadata (James Jean) → title, year, medium, dimensions, availability, optional story

## Patterns to AVOID

1. External commerce links → breaks immersion, loses sales
2. Fragmented presence → confuses fans, dilutes brand
3. Platform dependency → algorithm changes, fees, no control
4. Poor mobile UX → 60%+ traffic is mobile (Google data)
5. Zero artwork context → missed opportunity to connect with buyers
6. Static/dated design → need modern aesthetic with classic longevity

## Neo-one Differentiators

| Feature | Competitive Advantage | Addresses Pain Point |
