Summary

### Hero Section Design

- The team discussed multiple hero section concepts for the website entry point
- Initial concepts included: a TV screen design, a standalone eye graphic, and a room setup with various elements
- **Final decision**: An eye graphic that follows mouse movements on desktop (and taps on mobile) with a warning message for mature content
- The eye will be placed above the Neo One logo, without integrating the "O" into the logo text
- A parental advisory warning will appear to alert users about mature content (nude images, violence)
- On click, the eye will trigger an explosion animation transitioning to the main gallery/home
- Navigation will use symbols/emojis rather than text, prioritizing visual hierarchy

### Gallery Organization & Structure

- The gallery will display artwork organized by type/clusters first, then by year within each cluster
- Users will see previews of different artwork types (cards, comics, illustrations, etc.)
- Clicking on a cluster reveals a year-based carousel view showing artwork evolution
- Individual artwork details will include: zoom functionality (requiring a specific button click to access high-resolution 2000px images on a separate instance), technical specifications, and add-to-cart option
- The design accommodates both casual viewers and dedicated fans who want to explore work chronologically

### Design Specifications

- **Color palette**: The team reviewed two options - an acid green palette versus Nio's less saturated colors
- **Typography**: Gothic-style fonts are being considered
- **Visual separation**: A white border is needed to separate page elements from the black or white background to prevent visual conflicts
- The design needs to balance Nio's artistic vision across different types of work (colored vs black and white, serious vs cartoon)

### Site Features & Navigation

- Multiple entry points to the site are possible: direct URLs to calendar, artwork pages, etc.
- Main navigation includes: home/gallery, calendar, contact form, and artwork details
- The site will include a contact form for emails, purchases, and commissions
- Calendar navigation will be accessible from within the site

### Project Timeline & Process

- **Current week**: Finalizing low-fidelity wireframes and making macro design decisions
- **Next week**: Creating high-fidelity prototype in Figma with final visual design and micro decisions (colors, symbol placement)
- **Following 6-8 weeks**: Actual website development
- This week is critical for artistic and stylistic decisions
- The Figma prototype will be navigable and show the true website experience before development begins
