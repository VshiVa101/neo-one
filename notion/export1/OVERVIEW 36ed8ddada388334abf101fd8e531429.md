# Centralizing an Artist's Digital Identity

## **End-to-end product design for an emerging visual artist**

Building the first branded website for Neo-one to centralize his fragmented online presence into a cohesive digital home.

---

## 1. The Problem

Neo-one is a modern emerging artist without a solid branded online presence. His content is fragmented across multiple platforms (YouTube, Instagram, Discord), and his primary sales channel is Etsy—limiting independence , brand control and profit.

---

## 2. Project Goal

Create Neo-one's digital home: a branded website that **centralizes his online presence** and provides an **independent platform for business and branding**.

---

## 3. My Role

**Lead of entire product lifecycle** as UX/UI Designer, Web Designer & Solo Developer

| **Responsibility** | **Activities** |
| --- | --- |
| **Research & Strategy** | Stakeholder interviews, user research via Discord, competitive analysis |
| **UX Design** | User personas, journey maps, information architecture, wireframing |
| **UI Design** | High-fidelity mockups in Figma, interactive prototypes, custom asset creation |
| **Development** | Frontend development with HTML/CSS/JS (Antigravity-assisted), JSON-based CMS, responsive implementation |
| **Testing & Launch** | Usability testing, iteration, production deployment |

# **The M.V.P. Scope**

---

*The functional requirements the design must meet by Mar 8, 2026.*

To ensure a successful launch within the 9-week window, the scope is strictly limited to these **High-Value Features**:

- **🏠 Central Hub:** A responsive landing page establishing Brand Identity.
- **🎨 Dynamic Gallery:** A CMS-driven portfolio to showcase high-res artwork without code.
- **📅 Event Tracker:** A system to display upcoming streams/drops (solving the user's "FOMO").
- **📩 Commission Flow:** A structured contact section to professionalize inquiries.

---

**A responsive and branded 2-page website with 4 core functions**

| --- | --- |
| **Home Page** | Art Gallery • Brand Marketplace •Brand introduction, navigation hub |
| **CALENDAR** | Event Calendar • Contact System |
