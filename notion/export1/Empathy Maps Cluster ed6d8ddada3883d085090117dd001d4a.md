# User Groups Analysis - Neo Community

## User Groups Overview

| **User Group** | **Representative Users** | **Core Identity** | **Primary Motivation** | **Key Quote** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Apprentice** | Scazzo, Alexis, Charlie | Art students/aspiring artists seeking technical mastery | Learning techniques and validating artistic rebellion | "I copy his drawings to understand the technique - the line art, the chiaroscuro" |
| **The Seeker** | Enola, Sabrina | Introspective individuals seeking emotional/intellectual connection | Finding personal meaning and resonance in art | "It's like a puzzle to solve within myself - his art makes me ask questions about myself" |
| **The Resister** | MK, Scazzo (partial) | Anti-establishment ideologists | Validating countercultural views and supporting independent voices | "He says what everyone thinks but doesn't have the courage to say - against the capitalism in art" |
| **The Collector** | Charlie, Sabrina, MK | Materialists with financial constraints | Owning authentic pieces that validate identity | "I want something that feels like a piece of him, not just a transaction" |

## Key Insights for Website Design

### Cross-Group Universals

- **All groups** hate AI-generated art
- **All groups** value authenticity and "realness"
- **All groups** frustrated by social media censorship
- **All groups** want deeper connection than platforms allow

### Conflict Points to Balance

| **Tension** | **Group A** | **Group B** | **Resolution Strategy** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aesthetics vs Function** | Seeker wants immersive atmosphere | Apprentice needs clean technical view | Toggle/theme options or separate gallery modes |
| **Description Length** | Seeker wants deep context | Resister wants unfiltered rawness | Expandable descriptions with TL;DR |
| **Pricing Transparency** | Collector needs all details upfront | Artist may want pricing flexibility | Range pricing + custom quote option |
