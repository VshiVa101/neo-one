# User Journeys Maps

Owner: leo

## User Journey Maps (Aggregated)

*Mapping the path from frustration to the "Digital Home" solution.*

| **Stage** | **Discovery** | **Research/Deep Dive** | **Conversion/Action** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Marco (Apprentice)** | Sees a thumbnail on YT. | Tries to zoom on IG; gets frustrated by compression. | **Opportunity:** Use the new site for "Pixel-Level" study. |
| **Sabrina (Seeker)** | Feels a connection to a piece. | Searches for the "Why" but finds no descriptions. | **Opportunity:** Dynamic CMS pages with "The Story Behind the Art." |
| **Elena (Event)** | Checks Discord/IG daily. | Misses a drop because the notification was buried. | **Opportunity:** "Add to Calendar" button on the MVP homepage. |

| **Shared Need** | **Why Marco Cares** | **Why Sabrina Cares** | **Why Elena Cares** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mobile-First Design** | Checks habitually on phone | Lives on phone, constant companion | Phone is primary device for everything |
| **Uncensored Content** | Wants ideological freedom | Wants complete artistic vision | Wants authentic, unfiltered updates |
| **Direct Artist Connection** | Wants to collaborate | Wants to feel close emotionally | Wants to participate in community |
| **Independence from Platforms** | Hates censorship/algorithms | Wants unmediated access | Tired of fragmented announcements |

---

## How They Differ

| **Dimension** | **Marco** | **Sabrina** | **Elena** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary Lens** | Analytical + Ideological | Emotional + Intuitive | Social + Participatory |
| **Time on Site** | Quick, frequent (study sessions) | Long, wandering (immersion) | Quick checks (event updates) |
| **Success Feeling** | "I learned something" | "I felt something" | "I didn't miss anything" |
| **Website as...** | Workshop + Manifesto | Sanctuary + Gallery | Community Hub + Calendar |
