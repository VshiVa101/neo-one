# 📊 User Segmentation: Four Distinct Archetypes

*Based on affinity mapping of 7 user interviews, 4 user groups emerged with distinct motivations and needs*

---

## User Groups Overview

| **User Group** | **Shared Characteristics** | **Primary Goals** | **Core Needs** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Apprentice** | Art students/aspiring artists, 16-25 years, high technical interest | Learn techniques, validate artistic rebellion, participate in community | High-res zoom, process documentation, collaboration opportunities |
| **The Seeker** | Introspective individuals, 14-30 years, value emotional depth | Find meaning, understand artist's vision, immerse in artistic universe | Context/descriptions, evolution timeline, atmospheric experience |
| **The Resister** | Anti-establishment ideologists, 18-35 years, politically conscious | Validate countercultural views, access uncensored content, support independence | Uncensored gallery, early event notifications, unfiltered artist voice |
| **The Collector** | Materialists with financial constraints, 16-40 years, value authenticity | Own authentic pieces, feel personal connection, validate identity through ownership | Trust signals, transparent pricing/materials, handmade/personal touches |

---

## Key Insights

### Cross-Group Universals

- **All groups** hate AI-generated art
- **All groups** value authenticity and "realness"
- **All groups** frustrated by social media censorship
- **All groups** want deeper connection than platforms allow

### Strategic Observation

Many participants mapped to **multiple groups** (e.g., Scazzo = Apprentice + Resister, Sabrina = Seeker + Collector), but each had a **dominant archetype** based on their primary stated goals and behaviors.

---

## Design Implications by Group

| **User Group** | **Must-Have Features** | **Deal-Breaker** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Apprentice** | High-resolution gallery with deep zoom, filterable by technique/medium | **No zoom functionality** |
| **The Seeker** | Detailed artwork descriptions, thematic/chronological organization | **No context or descriptions** |
| **The Resister** | Completely uncensored gallery, blog/newsletter for raw thoughts | **Any form of censorship** |
| **The Collector** | Clear commission/purchase process, detailed product specifications | **Lack of trust signals** |
