# UX Research Analysis: Interview Clustering & Synthesis

## Executive Summary

**Participants:** 7 total (Sabrina, Enola, Scazzo, Charlie, Alexis, MK + 1 partial)

**Method:** Semi-structured interviews (30-60 min each)

**Date Range:** January 6-9, 2026

**Total Insights Extracted:** 180+ coded statements

---

## Critical Insights Overview

| **Finding** | **Severity** | **Evidence** | **Design Implication** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform fragmentation causes emotional friction** | 🔴 Critical | 6/7 participants | Centralized hub is validated need |
| **Inability to zoom/appreciate details is major pain point** | 🔴 Critical | 5/7 participants | High-res image viewer is MVP requirement |
| **Community intimacy is core value proposition** | 🟢 Strength | 7/7 participants | Preserve small-community feel in design |
| **Mobile-first consumption dominates** | 🔴 Critical | 6/7 participants | Mobile breakpoint is primary design target |
| **FOMO on events/updates is pervasive** | 🟠 High | 5/7 participants | Calendar/notification system needed |
| **Music is secondary to visual art** | 🟡 Medium | 4/7 participants | Music section can be Phase 2 |

## Cross-Cutting Patterns

### Pattern 1: The Intimacy Paradox

**Observation:** Users want to feel close to Neo **without invading personal privacy**

| **What They Want** | **What They Don't Want** |
| --- | --- |
| ✅ Process/work-in-progress content | ❌ Personal life details (family, relationships) |
| ✅ Artistic philosophy/thoughts | ❌ "Big Brother" surveillance feeling |
| ✅ Collaboration opportunities | ❌ Over-commercialization |
| ✅ "Behind the mask" artistically | ❌ Literal unmasking |

**Design Implication:** Content should be **personally curated by Neo** but maintain mystique

---

### Pattern 2: The Archive vs. Experience Debate

**Research Question #3 Answer:** Users want **BOTH**

| **Archive Needs (Utility)** | **Experience Needs (Entertainment)** |
| --- | --- |
| Chronological navigation | Visual impact on entry |
| Search/filter by style | Immersive artwork viewing |
| Complete catalog | Curated "best of" highlights |
| Historical progression | Surprise/delight interactions |

**Design Solution:** Dual-mode navigation

- **"Explore" mode:** Curated, immersive
- **"Archive" mode:** Searchable, complete catalog

---

### Pattern 3: Mobile Consumption, Desktop Appreciation

| **Device** | **Use Case** | **Design Priority** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mobile** | Daily check-ins, casual browsing, sharing with friends | Speed, touch targets, vertical scroll |
| **Desktop** | Deep dives, zooming on details, purchasing decisions | High-res images, multi-column layouts |

**Sabrina's insight:** *"Sul mio cellulare... ho paura che si perda un po'"*

### Gallery Requirements

**Critical Features:**

- ✅ High-resolution images (minimum 2000px wide)
- ✅ Lightbox viewer with pinch-zoom
- ✅ Dark background (black or very dark gray)
- ✅ No censorship warnings needed
- ✅ Filter by: Year, Style, Series
- ✅ "Evolution" view showing style progression over time (Sabrina's request)

---

### Shop Page Requirements

**Product Card Must Include:**

- Product image (multiple angles if applicable)
- Title
- Price
- Dimensions (exact measurements)
- Material/print method
- Edition info (if limited)
- Estimated shipping time

**Product Detail Page Must Include:**

- All of the above, plus:
- Large image gallery
- Story/description by Neo (optional but recommended)
- Shipping method details
- Return policy (if applicable)

---

### Visual Design Direction

**Color Palette Recommendation:**

- **Base:** Deep black (#000000) or near-black (#0a0a0a)
- **Accent 1:** Neon pink/magenta (#FF10F0) - represents vibrant work
- **Accent 2:** Stark white (#FFFFFF) - represents B&W work
- **Accent 3:** Neon green (#39FF14) - optional, for interactive elements

**Typography:**

- Custom handwritten font (provided by Neo) for headers
- Clean sans-serif for body (high readability)

**Layout Philosophy:**

- Minimal chrome around artwork
- Bold, high-contrast
- 90s web nostalgia elements (Sabrina mentioned this aesthetic and it was the first Neo’s vision)

---

### Mobile Optimization Priorities

**Given 6/6 users are mobile-first:**

1. Touch targets minimum 44x44px
2. Vertical scrolling (avoid horizontal)
3. Lazy loading for images
4. Simplified navigation (hamburger menu acceptable)
5. Gallery grid: 2 columns on mobile, 3-4 on desktop

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

Based on what users **didn't** want:

| **Don't Do This** | **Why** | **Evidence** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ❌ Open comment section on artworks | Could become toxic | MK/Alexis want "non-toxic space" |
| ❌ Require account creation to browse | Friction barrier | No one mentioned wanting this |
| ❌ Auto-play videos/music | Disruptive | Users want control |
| ❌ Replicate Discord on website | Discord IS the community | All users active on Discord already |

## Proto-Personas (Data-Driven)

### Persona 1: "The Detail Seeker"

*Representative: Sabrina, Charlie*

**Demographics:** 20-30, artist themselves, desktop + mobile

**Motivations:** Appreciate craftsmanship, learn techniques, collect meaningful art

**Pain Points:** Can't zoom enough, censorship hides details, fragmented portfolio

**Quote:** *"Quando vedo una sua opera mi ci butto a capofitto"*

**Design Priority:** High-res zoom, complete uncensored archive

---

### Persona 2: "The Community Member"

*Representative: Scazzo, Alexis, MK*

**Demographics:** 18-25, active Discord user, primarily mobile

**Motivations:** Feel part of underground culture, support artist, participate in events

**Pain Points:** Miss event announcements, want to collaborate, want artist proximity

**Quote:** *"È una cosa che ci lega a tutti"*

**Design Priority:** Event calendar, clear Discord link, collaboration opportunities

---

### Persona 3: "The Casual Fan"

*Representative: Enola*

**Demographics:** 16-20, newer fan, mobile-only, limited budget

**Motivations:** Discover artist's philosophy, casual browsing, share with friends

**Pain Points:** Doesn't always know where to find content, can't afford to buy yet

**Quote:** *"Mi piacerebbe vedere anche questa storia nel suo percorso"*

**Design Priority:** Easy sharing, chronological archive, free content (blog/process)
