# How Might We (HMW) Questions

Owner: leo

## Purpose

Reframe problems as opportunities for ideation in Week 3.

---

## Marco (The Rebellious Apprentice)

### HMW make artwork details so accessible that art students prefer Neo's site over Instagram for study?

**Decision:** Out of scope (post-MVP)

**Rationale:**

- Other Neo priorities take precedence
- Base functionality covered by implementing zoom feature on artwork details
- Future enhancement: dedicated study page showcasing artistic growth trajectory

### HMW celebrate the uncensored, raw nature of the work in a way that feels like artistic freedom rather than shock value?

**Decision:** In scope (MVP-compatible)

**Implementation:**

- Keep website uncensored
- Research hosting legal frameworks/exceptions

### HMW help aspiring artists feel like they're part of an anti-establishment movement, not just passive consumers?

**Decision:** Post-MVP (main scope expansion)

**Vision:** Become a model of freedom and authenticity

**Future roadmap:**

- Journal/paper fanzine to publish other artists
- Multi-channel collaborations
- Dedicated post-MVP page for collaborations

### HMW turn the gallery into a learning tool without making it feel like a tutorial site?

**Decision:** In scope (content strategy)

**Implementation:** Describe method and process in artwork captions/details

## Sabrina (The Immersive Seeker)

### HMW make each artwork feel like a conversation with the artist rather than a transaction?

**Decision:** Out of scope

**Risk:** Loss of content control

### HMW visualize Neo's artistic evolution in a way that feels like a personal journey, not a timeline?

**Decision:** In scope (content curation)

**Implementation:**

- Gallery of chosen capstones
- Description/caption for every image showing evolution narrative

### HMW help users imagine owning a piece before they commit to purchasing?

**Decision:** N/C (no change/neutral)

### HMW create an atmosphere that encourages contemplation and emotional exploration, not quick scrolling?

**Decision:** In scope (interaction design)

**Implementation:**

- Symbolism instead of heavy text
- Hover cursor features/interactions
- Thematic soundtrack based on user activities

## Business Goals

### HMW make the commission process feel exclusive and personal rather than transactional and commercial?

**Decision:** In scope (form design)

**Implementation:**

- Form with 3 tone options: Insulti / Leccate / Richieste
- Personal contact format before and after purchase

### HMW differentiate Neo's independent site from platforms like Etsy in a way that builds trust?

**Decision:** Post-MVP feature

**Implementation:** Post-purchase comment publishing (social proof)

### HMW balance artistic authenticity (raw, underground) with professional credibility (serious inquiries from publishers/organizers)?

**Decision:** In scope (brand strategy)

**Implementation:**

- Moderation on publisher selection
- Become own publisher
- No cookies policy (privacy-first stance)
